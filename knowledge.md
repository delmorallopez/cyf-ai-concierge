## About CYF

Code Your Future (CYF) provides free technical training and support to help people overcome barriers to entering technology careers.

Official website:
https://codeyourfuture.io/

## Learning pathway

The CYF website describes the following pathway:

### 1. Intro to Digital
- Self-paced introductory course
- Approximately 20–40 hours
- Covers introductory digital skills and building websites
- Suitable as a starting point for complete beginners

### 2. Intro to Programming
- Approximately 13 weeks, part-time
- Covers programming fundamentals, JavaScript, HTML, CSS and testing
- Builds on Intro to Digital

### 3. Checkpoint
- Approximately 3 weeks, part-time
- Practical assessment of programming skills

### 4. Software Development
- Approximately 12 weeks, part-time
- Covers software engineering fundamentals, systems and problem-solving

### 5. The Launch
- Approximately 5 weeks, part-time
- Focuses on developing a real product within a team

## Guidance for prospective applicants

- Ask about interests, experience and learning goals.
- Explain programmes in accessible language.
- For complete beginners, explain Intro to Digital as the published starting point.
- Explain that later courses build on earlier learning.
- Do not guarantee admission, employment or progression.
- Do not make final eligibility decisions.
- Do not invent course availability or deadlines.
- Direct visitors to official CYF pages for current application details.
