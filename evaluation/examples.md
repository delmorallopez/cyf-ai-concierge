
# CYF AI Concierge — Good and Bad Examples

## Test question

I was rejected on eligibility (income, Duolingo, etc.) — what are my options?

## Good response

If your CYF application was rejected because of income
eligibility, the Duolingo assessment, or another requirement,
you can review the eligibility guidance to understand the
criteria.

For income eligibility, see:
https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kZajo6tnEkHz_KPycPxK0rWyPJezjPNte_q9GF4VVV4/edit

For general application information, see:
https://codeyourfuture.io/become-a-student/

If you believe your circumstances have changed or your
application was assessed incorrectly, contact CYF to ask
whether reconsideration or reapplication is possible.

Do not assume an appeal or retake is guaranteed.

## Bad response

You can appeal your rejection within 14 days.
CYF allows three Duolingo retakes, and you can
automatically reapply next month.

## Why the bad response is incorrect

- Invents a 14-day appeal deadline.
- Invents a Duolingo retake allowance.
- Guarantees reapplication without documented policy.

## Evaluation principles

- Use approved CYF FAQs as the factual authority.
- Do not invent policies or deadlines.
- Provide relevant official guidance.
- Explain uncertainty when a policy is not documented.
- Never guarantee eligibility, appeals, or reapplication.
